# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients, hiring teams, and creative collaborators evaluating Emmanuel Tejeda's web-development and audio-production work. Visitors need to understand his capabilities, see concrete evidence, and contact him without friction in English or Italian.

## Product Purpose

Present Emmanuel's full-stack development and audio expertise through concise, credible work samples. Success means visitors quickly understand what he does, trust the evidence, and start a relevant conversation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, pragmatic, and cosmopolitan. The experience should feel precise and human, with restrained Italian warmth and direct language instead of self-promotional spectacle.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates, terminal cosplay, repetitive card grids, decorative gradients, shadow-heavy glass surfaces, low-contrast beige minimalism, and unsubstantiated claims. Do not let visual novelty obscure project evidence or bilingual usability.

## Design Principles

- Lead with proof: concrete outputs, responsibilities, and outcomes before broad claims.
- Make bilingual access structural, not decorative; English and Italian receive equal care.
- Keep the path from evidence to contact obvious and low-friction.
- Use Italy/New York context to orient visitors and convey personality without tourism clichés.
- Prefer production credibility: accessible interaction, responsive behavior, performance, and tested workflows are part of the portfolio story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks, sufficient contrast, text resizing, reduced-motion behavior, and equivalent English/Italian content. Interactive controls should remain usable on touch and narrow screens.
